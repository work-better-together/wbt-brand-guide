Case study carousels

Every case study on our site becomes a carousel that walks through its beats: the photo and title, The Challenge, Our Approach, what shifted, the client in their own words, and the key takeaway. Where a case study tells a beat in prose rather than a list, the slide uses its opening sentences, word for word. Each tile pages through its slides, as on Instagram: swipe, or hover for the arrows.

Stories

Stories at 1080×1920, with text kept out of the top 250px and bottom 340px where the app's buttons sit. Case studies tell the same beats as their carousels; each free resource gets a short series ending on the link. Stories carry less reading than carousels: about 30 words a screen, so a long beat runs over two frames. Three across, animated with Come Together: each frame's words gather line by line, hold long enough to read, then drift apart into the next. Press play.

The rules
  • Sizes. Headlines 64–92px, body 36px or more, eyebrows 30px, nothing below 26px on a 1080px card. Phones show cards at a third of their size.
  • Margins. 86px (8%) on every side. Text never touches the monogram: keep 24px clear around it.
  • Logo. The monogram, bottom right, 70px tall. Black on light grounds, Clear White on dark.
  • Collages. Spot, then scribble, then illustration, built from the real files. Scribbles stay black, so collage posts go on light grounds.
  • Color. Mostly solid brand colors; a watercolor wash now and then. On dark washes, a tint of the same color keeps text readable.
  • Words. Quotes and case study lines are word for word. Links go in the caption; on Stories, the web address and a link sticker.
  • Stats. Intentional Blue or Honest Ochre on light grounds, Radiant Gold on dark. Always name the source.
  • Motion. Posts can animate with Come Together: see Motion.
