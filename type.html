Typography

Three typefaces and a defined scale. Each typeface has a distinct voice; together with sizes, weights, and roles for every level of the hierarchy, they form a coherent, expressive system.

This page-header is the example Eyebrow: League Spartan 500, uppercase, positive tracking. Lead paragraph: League Spartan 400, roman, full ink. Supporting text under a page-level headline stays in the same family — Lora picks up at the next body level.

Don’ts · League Spartan

  • Weights are 500 and 700 for headings & labels, plus 400 for the deck/subtitle role only — no 300, 600, 800, or 900.
  • Never use Inconsolata for eyebrows or section labels — those are League Spartan 500.
  • Never set Lora for the lead paragraph under a page-level headline — that supporting text is League Spartan.

Don’ts · Lora

  • Never use italic for general emphasis inside a normal sentence — emphasis is bold, optionally with an intentional-blue scribble.
  • Never combine italic with color shift on the same span — pick one treatment.
  • Never use Lora for headlines (League Spartan) or attributions (Inconsolata).

Text markings & emphasis

Key words in running text get a hand-drawn “ink” mark instead of a plain underline. Use sparingly — one or two per passage, never every phrase. Blue is the default; gold is reserved for insight (matching Radiant Gold’s accent-only role). Circle and underline are drawn in on scroll (see Motion); wave, zigzag, and gold-scratch are static.
