On the philosophy
A piece of merch should feel earned, not branded.

Our merch isn’t marketing. It’s a tactile artifact of the work — given to clients after long engagements, worn by facilitators in the room, kept by the team as a reminder of why this matters. Quality over volume. Restraint over reach.
